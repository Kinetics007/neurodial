Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

NeuroDial is developed by David Carlson. This policy describes how the app handles information. NeuroDial is designed to work without user accounts and without advertising.

Summary

  • No user accounts or sign-in required
  • No third-party advertising or ad tracking in the app
  • Audio sessions run on your device; we do not receive your listening history
  • HealthKit data is used only if you grant permission, for features such as Mindful Minutes

Information we collect

Information you provide

If you contact us at dcarlson04@me.com, we receive the information you include in your message so we can respond.

HealthKit

If you turn on Log Mindful Minutes in the app and grant permission, NeuroDial may write Mindful Session samples to Apple Health for the time you are actively listening. NeuroDial does not read other Health data. Pause and stop end the current sample so paused time is not counted. We do not operate a server that collects your HealthKit data.

Data not collected by the developer

NeuroDial does not require a profile. App settings and session preferences are stored locally on your device.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share data with third parties for advertising.

Contact

Questions about this policy: dcarlson04@me.com
