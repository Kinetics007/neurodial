Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2025

NeuroDial is built with privacy in mind. We do not require an account, we do not show ads, and we do not sell your data.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information on our servers. NeuroDial runs on your device. Audio is generated locally. We do not operate a login system or cloud database for your sessions.

Apple Health (optional)

If you enable Mindful Minutes logging, NeuroDial writes mindful session samples to Apple Health on your device. NeuroDial does not read your other Health data. You control this permission in iOS Settings → Health.

Data stored on your device

NeuroDial may store preferences such as your last used frequency, theme, and timer settings using on-device storage (UserDefaults). This data stays on your iPhone or iPad.
