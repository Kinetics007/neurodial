What NeuroDial does

With headphones, NeuroDial plays slightly different tones in each ear. Your brain perceives the difference as a gentle rhythmic pulse—a binaural beat—that many listeners use for relaxation, meditation, or focus.

  • Live binaural beats from 1–50 Hz with Delta, Theta, Alpha, Beta, and Gamma presets
  • Eight atmospheres including ocean, rain, thunder, river, and more
  • Frequency sweep and warmth controls to shape your sound
  • Session timer with 15, 30, 45, and 60-minute presets
  • HealthKit Mindful Minutes when you choose to log sessions
  • Siri & Shortcuts, Lock Screen controls, and background audio
  • No subscription — full access included

Who it’s for

NeuroDial is for anyone who wants a simple, customizable sound session for sleep, meditation, stress relief, or focus—without juggling multiple apps or paying recurring fees.

Wellness notice

NeuroDial is a relaxation and focus tool for general wellness. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. Use headphones for binaural beats. Do not use while driving or operating machinery.
