Binaural beats for focus, calm, and restful sleep

NeuroDial is a wellness app that generates live binaural beats from 1–50 Hz, paired with immersive atmospheres. Tune your session for study, meditation, or sleep—on your terms, with no accounts and no ads.

What NeuroDial does

When you listen with headphones, NeuroDial plays slightly different tones in each ear. Your brain perceives the difference as a gentle rhythmic pulse—a binaural beat—that many people use alongside relaxation or focus routines.

  • Live binaural beats from 1–50 Hz with brainwave band presets
  • Eight atmospheres to shape the mood of your session
  • Frequency sweep and warmth controls to fine-tune the sound
  • Session timer so you can set a duration and unwind
  • HealthKit Mindful Minutes when you choose to log mindful time
  • Siri & Shortcuts, Lock Screen controls, and background audio
  • No subscription, no user accounts, and no advertising

Who it's for

Focus

Study, work, or creative sessions with adjustable brainwave bands.

Relax

Wind down with calm atmospheres and gentle frequency sweeps.

Sleep

Prepare for rest with low-frequency presets and a session timer.
