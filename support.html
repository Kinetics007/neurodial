Frequently asked questions

Do I need headphones?

Yes. Binaural beats work best with stereo headphones or earphones so each ear receives a separate tone. Speakers usually will not produce a clear binaural effect.

Audio is not playing or sounds wrong

Check volume and the Ring/Silent switch. Disconnect Bluetooth and reconnect if needed. Force-quit NeuroDial and start a new session. Make sure no other app is blocking audio playback.

How do Mindful Minutes work?

If you enable logging in the app, NeuroDial can save listening sessions to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes. You can manage permissions anytime in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → NeuroDial.

Siri, Shortcuts, and Lock Screen controls

NeuroDial supports Siri and Shortcuts for starting sessions, and Lock Screen / Control Center controls while audio is playing. Make sure NeuroDial has permission to use Siri and that playback is active for Now Playing controls.

Does NeuroDial work in the background?

Yes. Audio continues when your screen is locked or you switch apps, as long as playback is running.

Is there a subscription?

No. NeuroDial does not require a subscription. All features are included with the app.
