Frequently asked questions

Do I need headphones?

Yes. Binaural beats require separate tones in each ear, so stereo headphones or earphones work best.

Audio isn't playing or sounds wrong

Check volume and the Ring/Silent switch. Force-quit and reopen NeuroDial. Try reconnecting Bluetooth headphones.

How do I use Siri or Shortcuts?

Add NeuroDial actions from the Shortcuts app. Update iOS and NeuroDial if a shortcut fails.

Is there a subscription or account?

No. NeuroDial has no subscription, no user accounts, and no ads.
